## Stack

## Users

Students' client case: Maison Pierre Oteiza's social media audience. Primary target to win: urban 25-40 year-olds who love food and care about origin and traceability. Existing loyal base: 35-65 gastronomy lovers, Basque Country locals, tourists.

## Product Purpose

Maison Pierre Oteiza (Les Aldudes, Pays Basque, founded 1987 by Pierre and Catherine Oteiza; son Frantxoa joined) makes and sells artisanal Basque charcuterie. The communication goal: modernise the brand's voice and grow digital visibility with a younger generation without losing authenticity, artisanal know-how and territorial roots.

## Positioning

Premium Basque gastronomic house: exceptional product × Basque terroir × human story. Pierre Oteiza helped save the Basque Pie Noir pig breed from extinction (late 1980s); the Jambon de Kintoa obtained AOP status in 2019 and is cured 18 to 24 months in the collective drying room of the Aldudes valley. Pigs are raised outdoors.

## Operating Context

Instagram carousel posts (square 1080×1080) from the editorial calendar (pillars: Informer, Inspirer, Engager). Shops in Les Aldudes, Ainhoa, Saint-Jean-de-Luz, Capbreton, Biarritz, Bayonne, Bordeaux, Paris; e-commerce at pierreoteiza.com.

## Brand Commitments

Slogan: « Vivre et travailler au Pays Basque ». Products: Jambon de Kintoa AOP, Jambon de la Vallée des Aldudes, Jambon de Bayonne IGP, saucisson, chorizo, ventrèche, conserves.

## Product Principles

- Tell the story, not just the product: territory, breed, people, gestures.
- Modern format, authentic substance: real people and real gestures over trends.
- Inform before selling (80/20 rule).
